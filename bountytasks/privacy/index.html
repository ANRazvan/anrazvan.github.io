BountyTasks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

BountyTasks is a mobile app that lets small groups — flatmates, families, friends — post household tasks as "bounties" and earn points for finishing them. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, who else can see it, and how to delete it.

The app is made and operated by Răzvan Alexe ("we", "I"), an independent developer based in Romania. For anything in this policy, contact alexe.razvan05@gmail.com.

1. No sign-up, no email, no password

You never create an account in the usual sense. The first time you open the app it signs in anonymously and stores a random account ID on your device. We never ask for your email address, phone number or a password, and we have no way to identify you as a person from what we store.

2. What the app stores

DataWhy
Profile — the display name, emoji avatar, colour and time zone you pick So your group can tell who did what, and so reminders fire at a sensible hour
Content you post — bounty titles, descriptions, categories, loot listings, reactions, and any photos you attach It's the content of the app; your group members see it
Activity in your groups — points, coins, streaks, claims, completions, achievements, purchases of loot Scores, the leaderboard and the weekly recap
Safety data — reports you file and members you block So reported content can be reviewed and blocked members stay hidden from you
Advertising ID (Android/iOS), handled by Google — only if you choose to watch an ad To serve the optional rewarded ads and to prevent ad fraud

There is no analytics or tracking SDK in the app. We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, microphone or browsing history.

Photos

Photos you attach to a bounty, or upload as proof that you finished one, are stored in a private storage bucket. They are readable only by members of the group you posted them in, through short-lived links. Choosing a photo asks for camera or photo library permission; you can refuse, and the rest of the app still works.

3. Who can see your data

  • The members of your groups — everything you post in a group is visible to everyone in that group. Someone joining later can see the group's history.
  • Nobody else using the app. People outside your groups cannot see your name, your content or your scores. The app has no public profiles, no search for people, and no direct messaging.
  • Us — only to run the service, to investigate a report of abuse, or where the law requires it.

We do not sell your data, and we never have.

4. Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts the database, the anonymous sign-in and the photo storage. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob serves the optional rewarded ads and receives your advertising ID and general device information when an ad is requested. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play distributes the app and handles updates.

Reminders about your claimed bounties are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server, and we do not store a push token.

5. Ads

Ads in BountyTasks are opt-in and rewarded: nothing plays unless you tap "Watch an ad" to earn in-app coins. There are no banners, no ads on launch, and no ads that interrupt you. Coins from ads can be spent inside your group — they are not money, cannot be cashed out, and never count towards the leaderboard.

Because the app is made for teenagers, ad requests are configured for a teen audience: creatives are limited to a teen content rating, requests are flagged as being for users under the age of consent, and ads are non-personalised — they are not chosen based on a profile of your interests. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Deleting your data

Open Profile → Delete my account in the app (step-by-step, including what to do if you can't open the app, on the deletion page). This immediately:

  • removes you from every group you are in;
  • deletes your profile, your blocks and the reports you filed;
  • replaces your name on anything you left behind — past bounties, loot, boosts — with "Former member", so your group's history stays readable without naming you;
  • deletes your anonymous account.

This cannot be undone, and we cannot restore an account afterwards. Leaving a group without deleting your account works the same way for that group only. Deleted data is removed from backups within 30 days.

7. How long we keep things

Group content stays as long as the group exists. When the last member leaves a group, the group and everything in it is deleted. Abuse reports are kept for up to 12 months so that repeat problems can be spotted.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). The database enforces row-level security, which means the server itself refuses to hand a device data from groups it does not belong to — it is not something the app is merely trusted not to ask for. Photos live in a private bucket, never a public URL.

9. Age

BountyTasks is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are under the age at which you can consent on your own in your country (16 in Romania and several other EU countries), please use the app with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or erase it, or object to how it is used. Because accounts are anonymous, the reliable way to do all of this is from inside the app (see section 6) — we usually have no way to match a request by email to an account. If you write to us, please describe your group and display name so we can find the right data.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Romania that is the ANSPDCP.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the updated version is posted here with a new date at the top, and significant changes are announced in the app.
