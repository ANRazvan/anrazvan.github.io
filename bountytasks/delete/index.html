Delete your BountyTasks account

For the BountyTasks app by Răzvan Alexe. Last updated: 30 September 2026.

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open BountyTasks and go to the Home screen.
  2. Tap your avatar at the top to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

BountyTasks has no email or password. Your account is an anonymous ID stored on your device, so deleting it in the app is the fastest and most reliable way.

Can't open the app any more?

If you uninstalled the app or lost your phone, the anonymous account cannot be linked back to you by email, so I can only delete it if we can find it another way. Email alexe.razvan05@gmail.com with the subject BountyTasks account deletion and tell me:

  • the display name you used,
  • the name of a group you were in, and roughly when you joined.

I will delete the matching account and reply within 30 days. Reinstalling the app creates a brand new account; it does not bring the old one back.

What is deleted

Deleted immediatelyKept, anonymised
  • Your profile: display name, avatar, colour, time zone
  • Your membership in every group
  • Your reactions
  • The members you blocked and the reports you filed
  • Your record of watched ads
  • Your anonymous account itself
  • Bounties, loot and boosts you posted or completed stay in your group's history so it still makes sense to the others. Your name on them is replaced with "Former member" and is no longer linked to you.
  • Photos you attached to those bounties stay with the bounty, visible only to that group, unless a group member deletes the bounty.

Retention

  • Deleted data is removed from backups within 30 days.
  • If you are the last member of a group, the group and everything in it (including photos) is deleted with you.
  • Abuse reports about other people's content are kept for up to 12 months.
  • Nothing can be restored after deletion.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Leave a group: Group settings → Leave group. Your name on that group's history becomes "Former member".
  • Delete a bounty you posted (including its photo): open it and use the ⋯ menu → Delete.
  • Unblock someone: Profile → Safety.
  • Reset your advertising ID: your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
